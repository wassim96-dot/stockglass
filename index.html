Sources & method

  • Prices: Yahoo Finance through public relays. Statements: SEC EDGAR (US listings); consensus estimates: Nasdaq.
  • Market backdrop: VIX, VIX3M and S&P 500 from Yahoo; credit spreads: ICE BofA option-adjusted spreads (FRED, end of day, 1–2 days late), estimated live from the HYG and LQD bond funds vs Treasuries (IEF) with durations ≈ 3.2, 8.3 and 7 years; rates: US Treasury; inflation: BLS and BEA (BLS API, DBnomics or FRED; monthly changes from the index can differ by 0.05 pt from the rounded headline).
  • Fear score = 0.3 × variance risk premium (HAR model) + 0.7 × fear phase. It does not enter any score.
  • Short-term setup score = 0.5 × Minervini full check + 0.5 × technicals. In our 2012–2026 test on 98 large US stocks, higher scores had smaller dips, not higher 2–12 week returns.
  • Minervini: Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard (2013) and Think & Trade Like a Champion (2017); each ingredient has academic support, the full template has no peer-reviewed test.
  • Colours and "Positive / Negative" after a line are a plain reading of that line, not a score. ∅ = missing: excluded, never guessed. Tap any title or line marked ⓘ for a plain explanation of the measure and its levels.

Rule-based screen on public data, not investment advice.
